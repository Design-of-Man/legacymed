# Legacy Medical: website (legacymedicalpalmbeach.com)

Static site for Legacy Medical, Dr. Yana Shtern's concierge internal medicine practice in Palm Beach Gardens, FL. A Design of Man client build. Generated: never hand-edit anything in `public/*.html`.

## Build
- `python3 build.py --check` regenerates every page into `public/`, plus sitemap.xml, robots.txt, llms.txt, journal/feed.xml, site.webmanifest, **vercel.json** (headers, CSP with hashed inline scripts, redirects) and `_dev/redirects/` (cmedjupiter.com 301 maps). `--check` fails on banned copy (em dashes, stock AI phrases; list in build.py `BANNED`).
- Stdlib only. Vercel runs the same command (`buildCommand`) and serves `public/` only, so env vars (GA4_ID, VERCEL_ANALYTICS, GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY) take effect at build time.
- Gates before any push: `python3 _dev/preflight.py` (must print READY TO DEPLOY), `python3 _dev/verify.py` (axe + console + 404s + overflow on every page at 375/768/1440, light and dark; needs `pip install playwright` and axe-core from npm, path at the top of the file), `python3 _dev/hero_contrast.py` (text over the hero video).

## Where things live

- Search Console: `GSC_VERIFICATION` in build.py is an empty slot for the HTML-tag token (owner's token, the `content` value only); empty emits nothing. The domain is already verified by the DNS TXT record, so it is optional.
- Page copy: content.py. Legal: legal_content.py + npp_text.py (HIPAA notice, carried over verbatim; do not reword). Blog (served at /blog, /journal 301s there; the Python files keep their journal_ names): journal_content.py (POSTS + REDIRECTS). Redirect map: redirects.py.
- House capitalisation (client, Oct 2026): "Internal Medicine", "Nephrology", "Nephrologist", "Interventional Nephrology" and "Concierge Internal Medicine" are always capitalised in copy (slugs and URLs stay lowercase). No "no obligation" wording anywhere. Instagram section and footer icon come from `INSTAGRAM` in content.py (@legacymedicalconcierge). Google rating and quotes on the home page are a dated snapshot in `GOOGLE`/`GOOGLE_REVIEWS`, replaced live when GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY is set.
- Accessibility widget: UserWay (account `QQECAXmKWE`, the practice's own, carried over from the old site at the client's request, Oct 20, 2026), loaded after page load by public/assets/js/userway.js on every page; build.py `USERWAY_ACCOUNT` and the CSP allow `https://*.userway.org`. - Page templates: pages.py. Design system: public/assets/css/site.css (black and gold palette, tokens on :root). ## Rules that are easy to break
- Never invent facts, reviews, staff, stats or prices. Unconfirmed items live in `_dev/OPEN-ITEMS.md`, not on the site.
- Approval emails for new content (blog posts, page copy) go to Yan at nydialysis@yahoo.com AND info@legacymedicalpalmbeach.com, from hello@designofman.com (Nick, Oct 30, 2026). Nick may publish without waiting for a reply; `reviewed_by` stays empty unless Dr. Shtern says she reviewed the piece.
- No tracking on pages with a form (`track=False` in write_page for /meet-and-greet and /contact). GA4 is consent-gated and only loads when GA4_ID is set.
- Analytics (Oct 30, 2026). Vercel Web Analytics comes from `VERCEL_ANALYTICS=1` (Vercel env, read at build time): the tag plus an inline `window.va` queue stub (`VA_STUB`, always hashed into the CSP because vercel.json is committed from a plain build). Speed Insights is a separate opt-in, `VERCEL_SPEED_INSIGHTS=1`, off: it is usage-billed. Events, from one capture-phase click listener in main.js; each goes to Vercel and, after consent, to GA4, with `link_location` (the link's `data-loc`, else `assistant` inside the chat panel, else `page`) and `path` only, never anything typed: `call_click` (tel:), `email_click` (mailto:), `directions_click` (a Google/Apple Maps or Waze link), `appointment_cta` (a link to /meet-and-greet), `chat_open` (visitor clicks the chat launcher, once per page view). A confirmed assistant tour request fires GA4 `generate_lead` {form: "assistant"} as before, plus Vercel-only `form_submit` {form, path} via the `legacy:assistant-lead` DOM event. `hero_video_play` stays GA4-only. Do not rename `call_click` or `generate_lead`: GA4 depends on them.
- No self-serving review stars in schema (preflight blocks AggregateRating/Review).
- Hero: the full-bleed coastline film fills the screen, shown exactly as shot: no shade, gradient or colour filter over it (owner, Oct 2026), and no visible play button (a keyboard-only pause control stays for accessibility; the film autoplays except under reduced motion or Save-Data). The compact copy (kicker, h1, lede, two buttons, facts) sits low on the left in its own small dark panel, Elite-style. The transparent header uses dark text over the bright sky. No italic in the h1, no `poster` attribute on the video. `_dev/hero_contrast.py` must stay all-pass.
- The photo reel uses only real practice photos. The 2020 "Concierge Medicine of Jupiter" shoot shows an earlier location and unidentified people: keep it out.
- Domain and DNS (since the Oct 19, 2026 cutover): registrar GoDaddy, nameservers Vercel DNS (team thedesignofman), site served by the `legacymedical` Vercel project. Practice email is Microsoft 365: never change or remove the MX (`legacymedicalpalmbeach-com.mail.protection.outlook.com`), the root SPF TXT or the `autodiscover` CNAME. Resend (form email) owns `send`, `rsend` and `resend._domainkey`; the root also carries the Google Search Console verification TXT. No DMARC record on purpose. Unkind Designs no longer hosts anything for the site.
