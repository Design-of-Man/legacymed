Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Legacy Medical wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, a keyboard alone, magnification or voice control.

What we aim for

This site is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Every page is checked with automated testing at phone, tablet and desktop sizes before it is published, and the site is designed to be used fully with a keyboard. Motion on the site, including the video and the photo reel, stops if your device is set to reduce motion, and both can be paused.

No overlay widgets

We do not use accessibility overlay plugins. We build the pages themselves to work with the assistive technology you already use.

If something does not work

If you have trouble with any part of this site, please call us at (561) 834-9280 or email info@legacymedicalpalmbeach.com. Tell us the page and what happened, and we will help you get the information another way and fix the problem.

The office

Our office at 11380 Prosperity Farms Road, Suite 114, is on the first floor and is wheelchair accessible. If you need an accommodation for your visit, let us know when you call.
